ABOUT US

Where It Started

LLFitness began in 2015, when Lorenzo Lanzafame started working independently as a personal trainer. In 2020, the first LLFitness studio opened, creating a dedicated space for personalised training and professional support.

What started as personal training has since grown into a multidisciplinary training and wellness space, bringing together personal training, structured group training, physiotherapy, sports massage and nutrition under one roof.

Today, LLFitness continues to evolve around the same principle it began with: helping people train with purpose and stay strong, capable and active for life.

Beyond Fitness. Training for Life.

SERVICES

Personal Training

A personalised approach built around your goals, ability and lifestyle, with tailored programming, professional coaching and measurable progression.

Fitness Assessment

Our fitness assessment gives us a clear understanding of your current health, movement and physical capacity before beginning your training programme. It includes a health questionnaire, blood pressure check, Visbody 3D analysis, posture and mobility assessment, followed by cardiovascular, strength and endurance testing during your first training session.

The results help our coaches identify your starting point, highlight areas that may need attention and build a training plan that is appropriate, measurable and tailored to your goals.

1-on-1 Personal Training

Individual coaching built entirely around you. Your programme is based on your goals, current ability, training experience and lifestyle, whether you want to become stronger, improve fitness, change your body composition or simply move and feel better. Sessions progress with you, giving you the structure, guidance and accountability needed for consistent long-term progress.

Private Small Group Training

Train together with a partner, friend or small private group while still receiving personalised coaching. Sessions are planned around the different abilities and goals within the group, combining the benefits of personal training with the motivation and shared experience of training together.

Body Recomposition

Personalised training and nutrition support focused on improving body composition in a sustainable way. By building strength, supporting lean muscle and managing body fat, the programme is adapted to your goals, lifestyle and progress over time.

Athlete Strength and Conditioning

Performance-focused training for recreational, developing and competitive athletes. Programmes are designed around the physical demands of your sport, with a focus on strength, power, speed, movement quality and resilience. Training can also be adapted around competition schedules and existing sport-specific practice to support performance while managing overall training load.

Pre and Post Natal Fitness

Personalised training designed to support women through pregnancy and their return to exercise after childbirth. Sessions are adapted according to each stage, with an emphasis on appropriate strength training, breathing, movement quality and maintaining physical capacity. Postnatal training progresses gradually, helping rebuild strength and confidence in movement before returning to more demanding exercise.

Mobility and Functional Movement

Designed for those who feel stiff, restricted or limited in the way they move. We use mobility work alongside strength and movement training to improve range of motion, control and overall movement quality. The goal is not simply to become more flexible, but to help your body move more efficiently for training, sport and everyday life.

Boxing / Kun Khmer

Private Boxing and Kun Khmer training combines technical coaching with physical conditioning. Sessions can include stance, footwork, punches, combinations, defensive skills and pad work, alongside conditioning suited to your level. Whether you are completely new to combat sports or already have experience, training is adapted to develop skill, fitness, coordination and confidence.

Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy is designed to help manage pain, restore movement and improve physical function. Following an assessment, treatment may include hands-on therapy, mobility work and targeted rehabilitation exercises based on your individual needs. Whether you are recovering from an injury, managing ongoing discomfort or returning to training, the focus is on helping you move better and reduce the risk of recurring problems.

Sports Massage Therapy

Sports massage is a targeted treatment for muscle tension, stiffness and physical fatigue. Sessions are tailored to the areas that need attention and can support recovery, improve mobility and help maintain healthy movement. It is suitable not only for athletes, but also for anyone experiencing muscular tightness from training, work or everyday activity.

Nutrition Consultation and Tailored Nutrition Plan

Our nutrition service provides practical guidance based on your goals, lifestyle and current eating habits. Whether your focus is fat loss, muscle gain, performance or improving overall health, we help you develop a realistic approach to nutrition that can be maintained long term. Tailored nutrition plans can also be adjusted over time as your needs and progress change.

Visbody 3D Body Composition Scan

The Visbody 3D Body Scan provides a detailed overview of your body composition and physical measurements in just a few minutes. It tracks key data such as body fat, muscle mass, body measurements and posture-related information, giving you a clearer picture of your starting point and progress over time. The scan can be used independently or alongside your training and nutrition programme to support more informed goal setting and progress tracking.

Structured Group Training

Our Structured Group Training follows progressive 8-week phases focused on Strength, Conditioning and Power. Rather than stand-alone workouts, each phase builds on the last to give your training structure, progression and variety throughout the year. Classes are capped at six participants, allowing our coaches to provide individual guidance while maintaining the energy of training as a group.

01 02 03
Build

Build focuses on developing a strong foundation through progressive strength training. We work on movement quality, control and gradually increasing load to build strength, stability and confidence across key movement patterns.

Condition

Condition combines strength training and conditioning work through push, pull and integrated movement patterns to develop endurance, work capacity and sustained performance.

Perform

Perform is about learning to use your strength with speed and purpose. We focus on developing explosive movement, improving how efficiently you produce force, and building your ability to perform with greater athleticism and intent.
